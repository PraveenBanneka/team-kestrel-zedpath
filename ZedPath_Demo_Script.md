Live app: the workers.dev link in the submission form. Record on the phone with the screen recorder and microphone on,
one clip at a time; join the clips in Clipchamp and keep the total under 4:00 (about 520 spoken words).

## Before recording

1. Phone: Do Not Disturb, brightness up, Wi-Fi on. Open ZedPath and refresh once (loads the latest version).
   If you are logged in: **Me > Log out and clear this phone**. You should see the Welcome screen with the team line.
2. Record clips 01 and 02 (they include the sign-up). Then, without recording:
3. **Ask ZedPath**: send these three, one at a time, and wait for each answer:
   - `Which courses am I most likely to get into?`
   - `I came 2nd in the national Maths Olympiad in 2024. Can that help me get into Physical Science at Colombo?`
   - `I love building apps and maths. Which degrees fit me best with my results?`
4. **Courses**: search `physical`, press and hold **Physical Science, Colombo (013A)**; search `data`, tap **Data Science (136L)**;
   search `software`, tap **Software Engineering (099L)**; tap **Add to my list**.
5. **Groups**: open the **Physical Science** group, tick the rules, **Join** (asked once only).
6. **Me > Pick an avatar**: tap a robot.
7. **Journey > Open now**: tap **Follow** on one open notice.
8. Keep the app open (closing it clears the Ask answers). Practise once, then record clips 03 to 12.

## Joining the clips (Clipchamp, on Windows 11)

New video > Import media > select all clips > drag them onto the timeline in order > drag clip edges to trim silences
and the Ask waiting time > check the total is under 4:00 > Export > 1080p (portrait is fine: choose 9:16).
