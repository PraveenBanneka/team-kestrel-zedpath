# ZedPath: 4-minute demo script (narrated screen recording)

About 540 spoken words at a calm pace. Each section shows one thing no other tool does.

## Before you record (5 minutes)
1. Me > Log out and clear this phone, so the app starts at Welcome.
2. Have these ready: Commerce, Colombo, Z 1.4518, A Business Studies, A Economics, B Accounting.
   In "Beyond grades" add: School cricket · Sport · National · 1st place · 2024.
3. Phone in Do Not Disturb, brightness up, screen recorder on. Speak slowly; cut silences later.

## Tips
- If Ask is slow, keep talking over the thinking line and cut the wait in editing.
- Ask needs internet; record on Wi-Fi.
- Notification settings (Me) can replace the Groups section if time runs short: "you choose 7 days or 1 day before, morning or evening".
