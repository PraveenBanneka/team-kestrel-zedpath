## 2. Before recording (15 minutes)

1. Phone: Do Not Disturb, brightness up, Wi-Fi on. Log out and clear ZedPath (Me > Log out and clear this phone).
2. **Pre-run the Ask questions** (section 4) on the same account after registering: ask them once so the answers are on screen and you can scroll to them. Ask ZedPath keeps the conversation while the app stays open.
3. Add **Physical Science, Colombo** to My list before the Groups section, so its group shows under "Groups for you". Open it once, tick the rules and join.
4. Practise once without recording. Talk slowly; 4 minutes is about 520 spoken words.

## 4. Ask ZedPath questions (pre-run; show three, keep the rest as spares)

1. **Which courses am I most likely to get into?** (suggestion chip: shows chances and course buttons)
2. **I came 2nd in the national Maths Olympiad in 2024. Can that help me get into Physical Science at Colombo?** (uses his achievement: special intake, handbook p.166-167)
3. **I love building apps and maths. Which degrees fit me best with my results?** (uses his interests)
4. Do I need an aptitude test for Architecture at Moratuwa? (shows "Add to Journey" style next steps)
5. What is the interest-free student loan, and can I use it for a private IT degree?
6. Which government job exams can I apply for with my A/L results?
7. Sinhala: **මට Data Science කරන්න පුළුවන්ද?** (Can I do Data Science?)

The free AI quota is 30 questions a day per phone: pre-run each question once.

## 5. Recording and joining the clips (about an hour, no editing experience needed)

Record: on the phone, use the built-in screen recorder with the microphone on (record clips 01-11 separately; if you stumble, just re-record that clip). Send the clips to the laptop (USB cable, Google Drive or WhatsApp to yourself as a document so quality is kept).

Join in **Clipchamp** (already on Windows 11: Start > type "Clipchamp"):
1. New video > **Import media** > select all clips.
2. Drag them onto the timeline in order 01 to 11.
3. Click a clip and drag its left or right edge to trim silences and the waiting part of Ask.
4. Check the total time at the top of the timeline is under **4:00**.
5. **Export** > **1080p**. The video saves to Downloads.

If the phone video is tall (portrait), that is fine: Clipchamp asks for the aspect ratio; choose 9:16, or 16:9 with the phone screen centred.
