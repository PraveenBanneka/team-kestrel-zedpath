## Failures by root cause

1. **Muted text colour** rgb(122,131,150): 3.81 on white, 3.52 on page bg. Hits helpers, section labels, meta lines and unselected nav labels on every screen (A11Y-010, 013, 018-020, 022-024). One token change (to about rgb(94,103,122) or darker) fixes most.
2. **Green status pills**: '1 open' / 'N of 8 done' 3.08, 'Join' 3.03 (A11Y-019, 022, 023).
3. **Tamil (and Sinhala) band tabs overflow** on Courses: page scrolls sideways at 390px in Tamil (516px) and at 320px in Sinhala (336px). Tamil Paths 'See why' row overflows at 320px (A11Y-034, 035). Labels need to wrap or shrink.
4. **Small targets**: app-bar Me avatar 40x40 (A11Y-017); segmented tabs 42px high (A11Y-021); intro page dots 9x9 (A11Y-009, below even the 24px AA minimum).
5. **Welcome fineprint** (12px, white 72%) 3.66-4.01 where it sits over the teal glow (A11Y-006).

## Notes

- Course and university names stay in English in si/ta: data from the UGC handbook, not UI text. Counted as pass.
- Me's h1 is the Z-score ('Z 1.4200') rather than a page title; one h1 rule met, worth a look for screen-reader users.
- Tamil nav label 'எனது பட்டியல்' wraps to two lines (cosmetic).
- The server on 8899 did not answer for about 6 minutes at the start (several workerd processes on the port while other e2e suites ran); it recovered without intervention.
